# Identidad de Nuudo

Icono creado con el generador de imágenes integrado (`image_gen`, modo built-in).
El nudo entrelazado se inspira en las dos “u” de Nuudo y usa blanco sobre negro para encajar con la interfaz.

## Recursos

- `public/nuudo-icon.png`: icono de la interfaz, 256 × 256.
- `public/nuudo-favicon.png`: icono de pestaña, 32 × 32.
- `public/nuudo-apple-icon.png`: icono para dispositivos Apple, 180 × 180.
- `public/nuudo-social.png`: imagen original, 1254 × 1254, usada al compartir.

Las versiones pequeñas se obtuvieron redimensionando el original.

## Prompt utilizado

Use case: logo-brand. Asset type: production app icon for Nuudo, a business knowledge app connecting data, concepts and people. Create one original, striking monochrome app icon, square 1024x1024. Subject: a minimal geometric knot formed by exactly two broad interlocking U-shaped white ribbons, with a clear single over-under crossing. The double U in Nuudo inspires the mark; the silhouette should be compact, balanced, instantly legible at 24 pixels, distinctive and calm. Style: flat vector-like logo, crisp smooth curves, consistent thick ribbon width, clean negative space. Background: solid near-black #111111, full square bleed. Mark centered and occupying about 72% of the canvas with generous equal margins. Palette: white #ffffff and near-black only. No typography, no letters rendered as text, no wordmark, no slogan, no watermark, no mockup, no 3D, no bevel, no texture, no gradient, no shadows. Avoid generic infinity loops, chain link pictograms, floral six-loop knots, and any resemblance to existing AI company logos. Deliver only the finished icon, not a presentation sheet.
